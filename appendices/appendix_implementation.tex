NOVA, with their evidence, and the settings of the current configuration.

\subsection{NOVA}
\label{app:nova}

Three choices were made on the earlier release and are carried over.
Firstly, of background families of rank 2, 4 and 8, compared by blocked
cross-validation on the off-trace pixels, rank 8 was the smallest whose
worst-fold error lay within 5\% of the minimum. Secondly, the curvature factor
is common to the orders: its held-out $\chi^2$ was 3.26065 without a
correction, 3.21178 with a common factor and 3.15235 with one per order, but a
separate factor had to improve on the common one by at least 2\% and in each
order, but it improved by only 1.85\% and was worse in order~2. Finally, the
per-order scales $N_o$, the pixel layout and the off-trace pixel set were
fixed on that release. One choice has changed. The earlier release had an order-difference
term, an offset and a slope in log-wavelength added to order~1 and
subtracted from order~2, which was disabled by bounds of $\pm10^{-12}$; it is
now fixed at exactly zero, because at those small but nonzero bounds the
derivatives of the penalty terms were not exactly those of a model without
it.

The template $\phi_o$ of the spatial response is the median, at each
integration, of the fractional departures of its pixels from their own linear
out-of-transit baselines, detrended and scaled so that its deepest point is
exactly $-1$; the pixel cuts listed below apply to the template only. The
amplitudes are smoothed with a robust second-difference penalty across
columns, at a fixed rank of the pixel within its group, with the strength for
which the response smoothed on one fold best predicts the unsmoothed response
of the other. Negative values are then set to zero, the response of each
column is combined with the positive amplitudes measured in the wings of the
same column, and the result is renormalised within each group. The audit of
the earlier release found 14 centroid resets larger than 0.5 pixel and a
median registration difference of 0.234 pixel relative to the delivered
source, besides the red-end excess of Section~\ref{sec:nova}.

The policy files still list agreement tolerances between starts of 0.1 and
0.5~ppm, but the current closure does not test them. When no posterior sample
of positive weight is grazing, the upper end of the interval is set by a rule
of three on the Kish effective sample size, which is only approximate, since
nested samples are not independent. An ensemble realisation whose white-light
fit fails or whose starts are not strict is a failure, and in the two
block-length sets it stays in the denominator of 32. The reporting map has
rank 128 because the 147 bins are finer than the basis functions below
1~$\mu\mathrm{m}$ and above 2~$\mu\mathrm{m}$.

Several model classes were tested on the earlier release and are kept, with
the evidence against them, as negative results: free or priored differences
between the depths of the orders, continuum and order-contrast priors,
residual recentring, median source profiles, extra temporal background
modes, a narrow-response swap, local smoothing patches,
continuous-coordinate responses, explicit order-overlap modelling
and red-end transit templates.

\paragraph{Settings of the current NOVA configuration.}\label{tab:nova-settings}
{\footnotesize
\begin{description}[font=\normalfont\itshape,leftmargin=0pt,itemsep=0pt,topsep=2pt]
  \item[Support.] 97{,}557 pixels per integration in 2{,}720 groups; six edge groups without a reliable wavelength mapping excluded (order-1 columns 23, 24, 2043; order-2 columns 974, 1677, 1678); samples removed if not finite, with nonpositive uncertainty, or flagged \texttt{DO\_NOT\_USE}.
  \item[Spectral basis.] 160 top-hats over 0.62--2.84~$\mu\mathrm{m}$; the two above 2.76~$\mu\mathrm{m}$ held constant; transit evaluated on 5{,}454 wavelength nodes.
  \item[Orbit and transit.] $P=3.73548546$~d (public Ahsoka configuration); $\omega=90^\circ$, without effect for $e=0$; jaxoplanet quadrature of order 10; 21 Gauss--Legendre samples per exposure.
  \item[Limb-darkening reference.] STAGGER 6500~K plane (closest complete plane to $T_{\mathrm{eff}}=6550$~K), bilinear in $\log g$ and $[\mathrm{Fe}/\mathrm{H}]$ to 4.149 and $-0.25$; file fixed by content hash; scales 0.5 (logit, theory) and $2.0\,\mu\mathrm{m}^{-2}$ (curvature); no extrapolation beyond the grid.
  \item[Response template.] pixels with $\geq95\%$ valid out-of-transit samples, positive baseline, level above the 35th percentile of the stable pixels of the order.
  \item[Response fit and folds.] Huber threshold 1.345; $\tau_t$ from the mean out-of-transit time in units of its span; two alternating folds stratified across transit phases; accepted if the 95th percentile of the half-$L_1$ flux-distribution difference is $\leq0.15$ and centroids differ by $\leq0.5$~pixel.
  \item[Background.] calibration background plus $1,y,x,xy,y^2,x^2,xy^2$, orthonormalised on the 244{,}657 off-trace pixels; $G_1$: first principal component with $|\mathrm{corr}|\leq0.25$ to the mean of $\phi_o$ ($-0.0735$ on the earlier release); anchor from one joint weighted fit of all 16 coefficients.
  \item[Curvature factor.] geometric mean of the factors of the two orders, from trace pixels valid in every out-of-transit integration; fold means over the transit differ by $\leq250$~ppm; $\max|\gamma_t-1|\leq1000$~ppm; fold correlation $\geq0.95$ for real and injected inputs (175 integrations per fold on the real visit).
  \item[Uncertainty scales.] $N_1=3.0746905465$, $N_2=4.1917779190$; $s_p$ from two-way cross-validation of a level and slope per pixel on alternating out-of-transit folds, minimum one.
  \item[Closure.] maximum weight change $<10^{-3}$; relative objective change $<10^{-8}$; spectrum change $<0.01$ and $0.05$~ppm RMS on 40- and 96-bin summary grids; first-order optimality; stable rank; physical feasibility; confirmation cycle; no dense Jacobian formed.
  \item[Starts.] the second adds a fixed 250~ppm shape perturbation and small limb-darkening offsets to the first.
  \item[White-light priors.] $k_o\sim\mathcal N(0.1255,\,0.085)$; $u_1,u_2\sim\mathcal U[0,1]$; baseline level $\mathcal N(1,\,0.05)$ and slope $\mathcal N(0,\,0.01)$; noise multiplier $\mathcal N(1.4,\,2.0)$.
  \item[White-light sampler.] dynesty with 2{,}048 live points, multi-ellipsoid bounds, random-walk proposals, stop at $\Delta\ln Z<0.01$; failed emcee chains reached about 20--27 autocorrelation lengths.
  \item[Grazing mass.] 200 bootstrap resamplings with dynesty's own resampling; half-width of the 95\% interval $\leq0.01$; zero-count upper end $\min(1,\,3/\lfloor N_{\mathrm{ESS}}\rfloor)$; failure also if the stopping criterion is not reached; multimodality and tail diagnostics reported only.
  \item[Ensemble.] blocks start at a random out-of-transit integration and wrap within $[0,230)$ or $[600,720)$, so that longer blocks repeat frames; real ERR and quality arrays kept; covered if below the 95th percentile of $F$; fails if $\leq27$ of 32 covered (one-sided exact binomial, 0.025); Mardia skewness and kurtosis against 512 simulated Gaussian ensembles as a warning.
\end{description}
}

\subsection{The injector}
\label{app:injector}

The core projection, with a wing correction, a per-column flux
normalisation and a per-pixel out-of-transit response calibrated on the same
data, was made earlier in the project and is reused exactly; its spectrum is
held at its endpoint value outside its calibration interval, which only
affects the reads of integration~0, whilst the outer spectrum is linear over
all reads. The injector uses the public ExoTETHyS
code, except that the occulted flux is evaluated one planet position at a
time by a version which must reproduce the public routine bit for bit before
every run. In the three
transit tables of the pilot, integration~0 carried a round-off error of
$2\times10^{-16}$ in its visible fraction, which was verified to be harmless
and set to exactly 1 as a declared exception; in new tables any such
departure is a failure. NOVA's input is prepared with exoTEDRF 2.4.3, the version with which it has
always been prepared, whereas the exoTEDRF comparison uses 2.5.0. Because NOVA's
chain applies its $1/f$ correction between the reference-pixel correction and
the linearity step, the counts which its linearity step sees differ from those
used in the inversion by this correction. Besides the pixels outside the injected support, all
other extensions of the raw files and every saturated or inactive sample must
be bit-for-bit identical to the input, and the frames written by the $1/f$
step must reproduce the aperture sums of each order and column recorded
before and after it exactly. The second pilot
atmosphere was chosen, before any score had been seen, as the first distinct
case of the existing list.

If inspection of a recovered-minus-injected residual motivates a change to a
method, including a future repair of the spatial-response defect, the revised
method is evaluated on a new hidden injection before it contributes to an
accuracy claim. Because the injector and NOVA share the trace, wavelength and
kernel calibration, the programme will also include hidden injections with
deliberately mismatched profiles, wavelength maps, line-spread functions,
throughputs, backgrounds and temporal systematics. Every recovery is archived with
its inputs, code, configuration, diagnostics and scores.
