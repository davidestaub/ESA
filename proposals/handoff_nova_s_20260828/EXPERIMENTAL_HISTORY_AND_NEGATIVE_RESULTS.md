# Experimental history, decisions, and negative results

## 1. Why this history matters

NOVA-S was not selected from a single blind comparison.  Many model branches
were examined on one visit and injector family, and their truth scores informed
later scientific questions.  The frozen external visits, new atmospheric
spectra, mismatched forward models, and sealed evaluation are therefore needed
to protect the final claim from meta-level overfitting.  This file records the
successful and unsuccessful branches so that failed ideas are not repeated or
quietly omitted from the paper.

## 2. Early optimizer and support work

| Lineage | Main lesson |
|---|---|
| V26 | Cycle-2 failure exposed an invalid/unstable nonlinear coordinate. |
| V28-R2 | Best early usable run; approximately 203.631 ppm absolute RMSE. |
| V29A | OOT-median source/q-star profile did not solve the problem and risked embedding background structure. |
| V30-R8 | Clean Fold-B result, approximately 211.709 ppm; established cycle/TRF monitoring and two-start comparisons. |
| V31/V32 | Current PASTASOSS support and masks repaired the 1.03--1.10 um gap; V32 reached 185.668 ppm absolute and 163.566 ppm morphology. |
| V34 | Flux-conserving fair-cube writer reduced the result to approximately 171.176 ppm absolute and 143.503 ppm morphology. |
| V35 | Current-cube q-star family reduced morphology to approximately 128.685 ppm. |

These runs established three durable rules: inspect detector support before
changing the optimizer, use current instrument references, and monitor the
detector objective separately from truth RMSE.  Truth RMSE is not the optimized
quantity and need not decrease every cycle.

## 3. Order-offset and nuisance experiments

- **V33, free or priored order discrepancy:** rejected.  Independent order
  freedom weakened the shared-spectrum scientific constraint without a stable
  out-of-sample gain.
- **V36, per-column continuum priors:** catastrophic cycle-2 collapse.  This is
  not equivalent to response-amplitude calibration; it regularized fitted
  continuum coefficients rather than correcting the detector response.
- **V37, zero-centered aggregate O1--O2 contrast prior:** morphology reached
  approximately 127.407 ppm, but detector mismatch rose to approximately
  153.574 ppm.  The prior fought contrast genuinely required by the in-event fit.
- **V38/V40, constant recentering:** improved one aspect of the offset/morphology
  trade but was superseded by V50.  V40 achieved approximately 134.180 ppm with
  -3.127 ppm mean bias.
- **V39, wavelength-sloped residual recentering:** reproducibly regressed
  morphology to approximately 175.111 ppm and was retired.

The conclusion was not that the orders require independent spectra.  The
underlying detector responses and nuisance allocation required correction while
the shared physical spectrum remained scientifically preferable.

## 4. Response, background, and source-curvature evolution

- **V42/V45:** rebuilt source delivery and response bookkeeping around the fair,
  flux-conserving public cube.
- **V47:** refreshed q-star from the current public V45 cube, replacing the
  expired/stale response.  This materially improved global morphology and was
  incorporated into NOVA-S.  It later proved to carry an integer-coordinate
  red-end response defect.
- **V44, global sx4-to-sx1 response swap:** rejected because a global narrowing
  did not generalize across the detector.
- **Local sx1-like smoothing near x=768--800:** not promoted.  A fixed local
  wavelength/column patch would be visit-specific and truth-shaped unless
  generated by a visit-independent scene model.
- **V48, additional background temporal modes:** rejected by OOT evidence; the
  modes were not supported and did not provide a safe red-end repair.
- **E13b:** selected the 8-spatial by 2-temporal additive background through
  blocked off-trace cross-validation and low transit correlation.
- **V50:** added the common OOT-only source-curvature factor and became the
  frozen NOVA-S lineage.  Per-order curvature was not selected because it
  worsened O2 held-out performance.

## 5. Frozen NOVA-S and current reporting score

NOVA-S Science R1 achieved 117.830915 ppm absolute RMSE, +6.822111 ppm bias,
and 117.633258 ppm morphology over its original 143-bin domain.  The later R7
PASTASOSS training-domain reporting rule, applied without refitting, gives
115.973387 ppm absolute, +8.828131 ppm bias, and 115.636891 ppm morphology over
141 bins.

The headline strength is the combination of low global bias, good order-2
morphology, a shared spectrum, strict robust convergence, and a frozen
truth-blind calibration chain.  The principal residual weakness is supported
order-1 performance at the red end and, more broadly, O1 morphology relative to
O2 despite O1's greater nominal signal.

## 6. Order overlap: useful negative result

Recovery-side explicit ATOCA order-overlap modelling was tested in an overlap
branch and again in the R11--R13 transport campaign.  It did not improve the
red end and initially made O1 worse.  Direct injector analysis showed that only
2.145% of the red event signal is delivered through O2.  Removing this O2
contribution changed the decrement-depth ratio from 0.98906 to 0.98190: the
overlap was mitigating about 110 ppm of the bias, not causing it.  Explicit
overlap recovery is therefore archived as a negative result rather than part of
NOVA-S.

## 7. Settled red-end causal chain

The following results are established by the included audit products and should
not be re-derived without a new question:

1. **Self-closure:** a noiseless flat spectrum generated with NOVA-S's own
   response closes to about 1.3 ppm in the red.  Implementation, VarPro, and
   formal identifiability are therefore sound for a matched model.
2. **Fair-injector closure:** the same recovery on a noiseless V45 fair-injector
   flat retains -299.07 ppm red bias.  The dominant defect is deterministic
   forward-model mismatch, not detector noise or optimizer wandering.
3. **Operator split:** -167.36 ppm is present in the frozen response/operator
   before full inversion; -131.72 ppm is added when that mismatch is projected
   through the nuisance model and nonlinear transit inversion.  This second term
   does not mean VarPro is faulty: VarPro exactly solves the specified mismatched
   model.
4. **Q-star attribution:** the V47 q-star carries median attribution share
   0.999993.  The decrement cosine is 0.9965, but approximately 8.3% of the
   residual after scalar matching is spatially structured and only 0.32% is
   temporal.  Median centroid mismatch is 0.234 pixel; the response is about
   0.9--1.9% too strong at the faint red columns.
5. **Signal regime:** red transit-template S/N has median approximately 14.8,
   compared with 81.7 in the blue.  The existing red mask contains no empirical
   wing rows even though the injector delivers wing flux conservatively.
6. **Coordinate defect:** V47 smooths integer trace-relative ranks and shows 14
   centroid resets greater than 0.5 pixel.  The delivered V45 source shows six;
   the unsmoothed response shows 101.
7. **Amplitude diagnostic:** an OOT-derived per-column scale with median 0.98870
   reduced the direct operator bias to +0.57 ppm.  This identifies a promising
   model family but is not a promotable final result because the hypothesis was
   formulated after inspecting the known failure.

## 8. Red-end explanations ruled out

- TRIP versus STAGGER limb darkening: at most approximately 4.4 ppm per bin in
  the relevant audit, far below the defect.
- V50 source curvature: approximately 1.8 ppm effect in the red.
- Extra background/source cancellation: anti-alignment cosine 0.119 and only
  0.27% cancellation.
- Red field-star/order-0 spots: geometrically impossible at detector x<~700;
  supported red O1 lies at x=38--228.
- Random detector noise: bias persists in noiseless closure.
- O2-to-O1 overlap: small and bias-mitigating rather than causal.
- Generic optimizer failure: matched self-closure and two-start convergence
  reject this explanation.

## 9. Later response candidates

- **V51 continuous-coordinate registration:** removed many reset artefacts and
  improved red morphology from roughly 156.2 to 130.0 ppm and red RMSE from
  322.5 to 309.0 ppm.  It barely changed red bias and degraded O2 from about
  66.9 to 84.0 ppm and headline RMSE from 117.83 to 118.56 ppm.  Correctly not
  promoted.
- **V53 local transit-amplitude templates:** red amplitude estimation was too
  noise-limited; this reinforced the need for OOT calibration rather than
  event-local fitting.
- **V54/V54-R2 off-trace-background profiles:** best recovered profile shape;
  cross-dispersion residual approximately 3.10% versus 7.02% and fold centroid
  p95 approximately 0.032 pixel.  The family retained a signed centroid offset
  and did not pass all gates.  On the R7 global score, V54-R2 improved morphology
  from 115.64 to approximately 110.77 ppm but introduced approximately -38 ppm
  bias.  It is evidence that shape can improve while calibration worsens.
- **F277W validation:** independently supports the red O1 profile/centroid/wing
  investigation after coordinate conversion.  It does not supply a transit
  amplitude and cannot itself correct the spectrum.

## 10. What has not yet been cleanly tested

The remaining distinct response experiment is not “another arbitrary q-star.”
It is the full V54-R2 continuous-coordinate, flux-conserving profile combined
with one global OOT-only per-column response-amplitude rule, fixed before any
transit score is read.  The rule must be trained and checked in A/B folds, improve
held-out OOT detector likelihood, apply to every eligible column without a
red-local exception, and preserve O2/headline performance.  If this sealed test
fails, further red-end tuning should stop.

## 11. Other open model improvements

Correlated column noise remains relevant to morphology but cannot cause the
noiseless red bias.  A VarPro-eliminable per-integration column offset, anchored
by off-trace/other-order pixels, is the cleanest recovery-side experiment.
Automatic field-star/order-3 scene modelling remains required for cross-visit
robustness, especially near x~768--800, but it is not a red-end remedy for this
visit.  Both must be trained without truth and evaluated first on held-out OOT
data.
