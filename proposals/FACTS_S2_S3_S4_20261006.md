- **3.6 traces.** The red shift (+493 exoTEDRF, +515 NOVA) is in LOG l.112 and l.102.

- **3.10 reworded.** The unidentifiable quantity is light flat along each column, which cannot be assigned to star or sky from WASP-17 data alone. The products' far-row levels are background-subtracted, so they give no cap (board 1744Z/1746Z; blank-field test undecided, 0026Z/0043Z). The transit-shaped electronic term (ASTRA 1949Z) is kept as the reason the dip cannot decide it. ASTRA to confirm.
- **4.7 / 4.8 replaced by the built template** (`checks/E_template/RESULT.json`; ASTRA 1528Z):
  - 13 normal-field sources removed from the star image;
  - 18 offset-field components restored to the carrier side;
  - the largest crosses order 1 at x 484–1238, at up to 0.77% of the column's box flux;
  - bin 109 (1.874–1.893 µm) carries 7,195 light-ppm of non-transiting light, i.e. 108 depth-ppm at a 1.5% depth.
  - Do not use the 1258Z survey numbers (48 sources, 0.62%, 4.5 DN/s) in the ESA.
- **4.5 sky.** F_skyfix gives ε = +0.22 ± 0.99%; the earlier −1.0 ± 0.5% step was not a detection. Write "about 1%".
- **4.9 far halo.** 1258Z l.43 (band averages over 40–200 rows from the 4476 template: 1.3–1.5 DN/s blue, 0.2–0.4 red) does trace. Fable's per-row law per unit column flux is 4.7e-4, 1.3e-4 and 4e-5 per pixel at 40, 100 and 200 rows (blue), and 1.5e-4 ± 0.7e-4 at 100 rows (red, F277W). - **4.18 (new) declared absences:**
